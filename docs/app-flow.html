Inside one round, in order

no_buffraise_attackraise_hpraise_player_hpincrement_attack invulnerableno_damage attackreflect_damagereflect_damage_to_playerdeal_damage_to_all_opponent_cardsself_damage healheal_player dieresurrect deal_damage_to_opponent_playerdeal_damage_to_player
buffsprotectiondamagehealingdeath
Each round: every phase runs for player 1's slots 1 to 5, then player 2's, then an event loop replays the phases for queued reflects and death effects. Up to 20 rounds; round 1 applies the +20 % tribe bonus.
Finale: a lethal blow lets the whole last round play (bars go negative, cards stay), then the starburst and the banner. Any playback exception falls back to Skip.

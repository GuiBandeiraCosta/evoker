# Level-data gaps per card

Each card should have its level-1 value plus the value at the level cap of each fusion stage (base cap, +, ++, +++, ++++) for attack, HP and ability.
Level-1 values and level caps are known for every card. Not counted as gaps: stats that are 0 or fixed (do not grow), and the three trainer cards.

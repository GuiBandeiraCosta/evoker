- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RGizuAsD8jc (EvokerBr, uploaded 2017-05-30, 5:13, Brazilian-Portuguese client, 360p).

  - Text: "Troca vida e ataque de uma carta aleatória." = "Swaps life and attack of a random card." Matches the wiki.
  - Shown card is Goblin Thieves++ (Incomum, level 18): attack 1151 / health 1151 / mana 10. The attack and health are equal, so the swap is only visible on a card with unequal stats.
  - Level 16 (0:03:13 of video time on the sheets): 1059/1060. Level 19: 1198/1199.
  - The ability text names no side, timing or round.
- The "card" is also an event stage: it is the boss title in the event quest map ("Bossfight 4: Goblin Thieves"). It is placed in the middle slot of the enemy row. Boss HP is shown as a player-style bar (1450, 2000, 3500, 5250), so the event bosses are single-card-plus-escort fights.
  - Frame `RGizuAsD8jc_t0056_goblin-thieves-boss-fight.jpg` shows the fight, with a purple swirl effect on the enemy middle card around 0:55-0:58.
  - Numbers are unreadable (360p) and the player's cards are far overlevelled (12000 HP, attacks 4000+). No usable swap before/after numbers could be read.
- Event screens: the event banner (frame `RGizuAsD8jc_t0003_casino-event-banner.jpg`) reads "Dungeon Casino – O que acontece na masmorra, fica na masmorra" ("what happens in the dungeon stays in the dungeon"), with a "Portal closes in" timer.
  - Story dialogue: "Os anões guardaram tudo no cassino deles" ("The dwarves kept everything in their casino"); "O que acha de irmos lá dar uma olhada?!".
  - The rewards of quests are card pieces. Blunderbuss Gunner (Dwarf, Padrão, 300 atk / 100 hp / mana 4) appears as a reward card; it is not a Casino card.
- Confidence: high for the text. Nothing on mechanics beyond the text.

== Special Ability ==
In the first round, Goblin Mob's attack power increases by twice the ''basic'' power (that is, without any strengthening effects). Unlike implied by the text of the card the bonus is not a multiplier but an additive bonus two times - as indicated by the card modification sequence before the battle.

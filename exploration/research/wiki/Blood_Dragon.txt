== Special Ability ==
In combat, Blood Dragon has no attack or special ability. Due to its low mana cost, it can be used as [[Gremlin]].

When sacrificed to train another creature, Blood Dragon boosts the trainee's experience enormously. The exact amount of experience gained is hard to quantify (there is no numeric indicator in the game). Official [http://forums.flaregames.com/topic/11154-special-easter-event/ Flaregames forum] mentions "~20000 xp", although it is not clear what that means. As a practical measure, Blood Dragon trains a legendary++ level 24 card to level 32 (of 34).

Blood Dragon can't be fused.

== History ==
The only way to obtain Blood Dragon so far has been to get it (already at level 15) from the special Easter boosters during [[Unique Events|Easter 2016 and 2017]].

== Special Ability ==
In combat, Crystal Ghost has no attack or special ability. Due to its low mana cost, it can be used as [[Gremlin]].

When sacrificed to train another creature, Crystal Ghost boosts the trainee's experience enormously. The exact amount of experience gained is hard to quantify (there is no numeric indicator in the game). Official [http://forums.flaregames.com/topic/11154-special-easter-event/ Flaregames forum] mentions "~9000 xp", although it is not clear what that means.

Crystal Ghost can't be fused.

== History ==
A level 14 unfused Crystal Ghost was given as a gift during [[Unique Events|Easter 2016]]. It was also possible to obtain him in special Easter boosters. Since then, there has been no other possibility to obtain this card.

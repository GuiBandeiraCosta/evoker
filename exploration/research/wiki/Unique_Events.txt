== Easter 2016 ==
[[File:Are you ready for Easter.jpg|thumb|230x230px]]
This event introduced a new concept: Trainer (or Feeder) cards, which provide enormous amount of experience when sacrificed for card training, while being useless in battle. The new cards were:
* [[Lich King]]
* [[Crystal Ghost]]
* [[Blood Dragon]]
This was the first (and so far the only) way how to fasten the tedious card training process. However, the trainer cards were obtainable almost exclusively for real-world money.

The event lasted from March 25 to 28, 2016.

There was a special Easter booster. Each such booster contained:
* 1 ultimate card
* 4 trainer cards (one of which was [[Blood Dragon]])
* 4 legendary cards (including cards from portals)
Cards from Easter booster could only be get for Easter tokens: [[File:Easter_token.png]]. The Easter tokens could only be get as daily gifts or bought for real-world money.

Every day, each player received one of the following gifts, in the listed order:
* [[Lich King]]
* Easter Token 
* [[Crystal Ghost]]
* Easter Token

== Special Ability ==
The first time Enormous Larva dies, it is revived (i.e. returns to life with full health) with its base attack (i.e. without any strengthening effects) multiplied by 11.

If the Larva is revived by [[Moon Dragon]], the Larva cannot use it's own ability to revive again.

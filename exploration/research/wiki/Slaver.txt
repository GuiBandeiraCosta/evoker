== Special Ability ==
When the creature directly opposite Slaver dies, that creature's left and right neighbors are [[daze]]d for the following round. This ability persist regardless the <span style="font-weight:normal;">Slaver dies in given round or not.</span>

[[Sky Guardian]] has a similar ability.

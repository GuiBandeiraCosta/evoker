== Special Ability ==
At the end of a round, if Giant Spider's life is above zero, it will heal itself by some amount for each enemy card that died that round.

Example 1: A level 1 Giant Spider is hit for 1600 damage. At the end of the round three enemy cards die, so Giant Spider heals 1200 life (<span title="ability" style="border-bottom:1px dotted #000;">400</span> * <span title="# of dead opponents" style="border-bottom:1px dotted #000;">3</span> = 1200).

Example 2: A level 1 Giant Spider is hit for 2500 damage. At the end of the round two enemy cards die, but since Giant Spider is below 1 life it does not heal.

== Known Bugs ==
Occasionally, [[Devourer]] uses its healing ability even if it did not survive the round. Sometimes this is enough to keep it alive though it should have been dead. Oddly enough, this bug has not been observed with Giant Spider.

== Special Ability ==
Carnivorous Toadstool's attack doesn't trigger "reflecting" abilities of [[Loremaster]], [[Star Seer]], [[Lava Toad]], [[Will-o'-the-Wisp]] and [[Lookalike]].

The attack has no effect against creatures that are [[:Category:Indestructible|indestructible]], that is, it behaves normally with that respect.

== Questions & Answers ==
Q: What happens when Carnivorous Toadstool becomes suspended?

A: I assume that it will do the same it always did, because it's ability is not activatable.

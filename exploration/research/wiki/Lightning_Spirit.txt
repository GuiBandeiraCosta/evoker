== Special Ability ==
Lightning Spirit's damage dealing ability doesn't trigger "reflecting" abilities of [[Loremaster]], [[Star Seer]], [[Lava Toad]], [[Will-o'-the-Wisp]], [[Lookalike]] and player life.

The ability has no effect against creatures that are [[:Category:Indestructible|indestructible]], that is, it behaves normally with that respect.

== Tactics ==
<p>Lightning Spirit is a less powerful, safer and far easier to obtain variant of [[Wilderness Shaper]].</p>
<p>Lightning Spirit doesn't attack player's life. If all its teammates are destroyed, it is helpless against the opponent's higher life total. This is often exploited against it.</p>
<p>Lightning Spirit is susceptible to suspension, which completely disables it for one round. </p>

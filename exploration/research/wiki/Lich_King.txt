== Special Ability ==
In combat, Lich King has no attack or special ability. Due to its low mana cost, it can be used as [[Gremlin]].

When sacrificed to train another creature, Lich King boosts the trainee's experience enormously. The exact amount of experience gained is hard to quantify (there is no numeric indicator in the game). Official [http://forums.flaregames.com/topic/11154-special-easter-event/ Flaregames forum] mentions "~4000 xp", although it is not clear what that means.

Lich King can't be fused.

== History ==
A level 13 unfused Lich King was given as a gift during [[Unique Events|Easter 2016]]. It was also possible to obtain him in special Easter boosters or leveling up. Since then, some players have received this card at the game onset.

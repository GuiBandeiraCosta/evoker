== Special Ability ==
In odd rounds, the opposing creature and Thief's own left and right neighbors are [[Stun|stunned]]. In even rounds, the opposing left and right creatures are [[Stun|stunned]].

Every round, Thief's attack power permanently increases by the amount listed in his ability description.

== Special Cases ==
If the Thief of Time is suspended or stunned, it behaves differently.

Example 1: A [[Tundugar Ironeye]] opposite a Thief of Time. Tundugar suspends the Thief, and Thief will suspend Tundugar and its two neighbors in the first round.

Example 2: A [[Magmamancer|Magmamancer++++]] at an angle to Thief will stun it, but Thief will still suspend the cards opposite and next to it in round 1.

Example 3: It is a good tactic to place Tundugar Ironeye next to it. Though it will stun the iron eye but iron eye will be successful in suspending its opponent.

For another example, see Tactics below.

== Tactics ==
Two Thieves next to each other have the following effect: in odd rounds, the two opposing creatures, the two neighbors of the Thieves and the Thieves themselves are [[Suspend|suspended]] (hence the Thieves are not strengthened). In even rounds, the two opposing creatures and their two neighbors are [[Stun|stunned]] and Thieves are strengthened. In particular, this creates a permanent suspension wall in front of the two Thieves. Also the neighbors of Thieves are in a very advantageous position for creature-to-creature combat. On the other hand, the soft spot are the Thieves themselves, who will eventually fall if facing a powerful creature, hopefully buying enough time for the rest of the army.

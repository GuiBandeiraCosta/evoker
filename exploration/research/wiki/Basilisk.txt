== Special Ability ==
The card directly opposite Basilisk is [[daze|dazed]] for the first ''two'' rounds.

== Tactics ==
Due to it's unique ability, Basilisk is one of the most popular cards in the game. It can be easily obtained, trained and fused on the early quests and stays useful till 70-80th level - even to reduce spending mana during PvP battles. 

Basilisk is good in: 
* destroyng drilling type of decks by dazing strong attackers such as [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Grimnack Grimnack] or even "invulnerable" [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Mountain_Yeti Mountain Yeti] ++++. 
* neutralizing popular combo with "[http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Loremaster Loremaster] - [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Bloodsoaked_Totem BloodsoakedTotem] - Loremaster": till Totem is dazed, all three cards are harmless. 
* neutralizing [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Category:Indestructible indestructibles]
* dazing [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Moon_Dragon Moon Dragon] (Basilisk's damage isn't enough to kill MD and trigger his resurrect ability)
Common strategy with Basilisk is using two cards on the side of the deck (positions 1-2 or 4-5). In this case the one of Basilisks (1 or 5) is covered from side attackers by his neighbour (2 or 4). So player can use strong version of Basilisk on 2nd or 4th positions and weak (even not trained at all) version on the side flank - it will work well. This is the long but effective way to train the second Basilisk well without spending valuable resources. 

Using Double Basilisks gives economy of mana: decks value decreases from 105-125 points per battle (with 5 Legendaries in the deck) to 81-93 points. But this strategy needs much intuitive skill to avoid suspenders, card damagers and other dangers. Also player need strong attacking combo (e.g. drilling set) in other positions to end battle in 2nd or 3rd rounds.

Basilisk has no defence against:
* susperder attackers ([http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Tundugar_Ironeye Tugundar], [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Scorpion_Rider Scorpion Rider])
* side attackers ([http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Imperial_Dragon Imperial Dragon], [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Tulzakk_Bone_Grinder Tulzakk])
* card damagers ([http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Lightning_Spirit Lightning Spirit], [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Wilderness_Shaper Wilderness Shaper], [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Sand_Kraken Sand Kraken], [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Tribe_Avenger Tribe Avenger], [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Sun_Dragon Sun Dragon])
Nevertheless, the ++++ version of Basilisk (even fused without evolving each card) has approximately 2700 health points and is strong enough to withstand one hit of Imperial Dragon +++ during Round 1 (till it has no strengthen bonus).

Basilisk works good against [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Category:Direct_Damager direct damagers], which ability is triggered by death ([http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Ore_Titan Ore Titan] and [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Mammoth_Citadel Mammoth Catadel]), but it is helpless against other attacking direct damagers, such as [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Voodoo_Witch Voodoo Witch], [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Salt_Smuggler Salt Smuggler] or well evolved [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Homunculus Homunculus].

After level 80th advantage of using Basilisks decreases as opponents start to use well-fused ++++ side attackers and Ultimates.<!--VERTICAL WHITE SPACE BELOW-->

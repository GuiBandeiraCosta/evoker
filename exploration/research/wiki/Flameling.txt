== Description ==
The Flameling has a forking attack pattern that spares the direct opponent - which might be useful - and does some extra damage when he dies. In this respect he could be seen as a tiny Warg Rider without the devastating final blow. However, with its small attacking potential, the Flameling is not up for advanced fighting.

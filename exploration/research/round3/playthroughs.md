## 1. Discrepancies vs. our recreation (most important first)

1. **Gold is paid per tap, with a "PERFECTO / PERFEITO" popup and a floating number; it is random per tap, not a flat per-wave 55 % roll.**
   Qg @ ~13:00-13:30 (qg_q97_hud_strip.jpg): gold 12120 -> 12144 -> 12156 -> 12246 -> 12264 -> 12382 -> 12408; the floating "36" matched +36.
   B5 @ 0:10-1:45 (first quests): gold 240 -> 258 -> 294 -> 342 -> 378 -> 408, then 408 -> 462 -> 546 -> 576 -> 676. Coin bursts appear on some taps.
   The progress bar moves about 4-9 % per tap (Qg: 52, 57, 61, 65, 70, 74, 83, 87, 100 %). Confidence high.
2. **Stamina per tap at Q97 is 7, not 8.** Qg HUD strip: 202 -> 195 -> 188 -> 174 -> 167 -> 153 -> 146 (double steps are two taps). Our quests.json Q97 has staminaPerTap 8. High confidence for that node.
3. **Early quests cost 2-3 stamina per tap, and total cost differs from ours.** Tv @ 0:30-0:45: quest 1 = 50 -> 40 = 10 stamina and 200 -> 240 gold (+40) (we: 15 stamina, ~70 gold). B5: quests drain 50 -> 32 (18) and 32 -> 15 (17) with +168 and +268 gold. Medium confidence (v1.7.0 tutorial may be tuned differently from the main-map data).
4. **Level-up has a reward panel.** B5 @ ~3:30 "Sube nivel / Nivel 2 alcanzado / Premios / Puntos: 9 /12" with HP 1530, stamina 51, mana 51, +/- buttons, "Aceptar" (b5_lvl2_shop.jpg). Level 2 reward = 100 gold + gems (gems 0 -> 5; gold 688 -> 788). Level 3 reward = a stamina potion icon ("Puntos: 0 /12", HP 1740, stamina 59, mana 57; potion counter later "2 pocimas", then "3 pocimas"). Level 6 panel is a big card-style "Sube nivel" for a card (4 -> 5, attack 322 -> 354, HP 161 -> 177). Our design has +12 points and refill but no gold/gem/potion reward. Points per level = 12 (confirmed "/12"). Refill confirmed: stamina max after each level 55/59/63/67/71 and mana 53/57/61/65/69 with full bars.
5. **Tutorial/first map in v1.7.0.** Start screen shows the version "1.7.0" and the first map is the portal-style map "DRAGON HUNT" with a banner timer `11:02:05:10`, nodes 1.., ranger guide with bubbles. First fight is vs **Worm Shaman** (300 HP at first, player 1500), card "Orc Grunt" is added ("Carta anadida al mazo: Orc Grunt 0/8"), the deck screen has locked slots 1, 4, 5 (3 locks), only 2 cards at first ("Tus cartas 3/60", "Mana requerido: 5 / 50"). Our tutorial (design section 9) uses Pouch Tearer on Q1 and slots 2-4 open. Slots in 1.7.0: lock icons on slots 1 and 5 (and 4) at the start; slot 2-3 usable; more slots appear by level 3-4 (deck had 4 cards at lvl 3, 5 at lvl 4).
6. **Feature unlock levels differ.** Bottom bar locks seen: first two buttons (trophy, arena) locked at the start; cards button opens early; Arena (crossed swords) available from about level 4; League ("Ligas activadas - Llega a la cumbre!" popup, trophy button) at **level 6** in 1.7.0 (we: Arena 4, League 5). Training ("Entrenamiento activado: Sacrifica cartas debiles y mejora las fuertes.") unlocks around level 5 (B5 @ ~25:30).
7. **XP is shown only as a bar.** No XP numbers on any screen; the small digit in front of the stamina/mana bars is the unspent-point tier (1, 2, 3, 0), not XP. Our `100*L^1.4` stays unverifiable.
8. **Player HP growth is allocation-driven**: level 50 account has 5,280 HP, 262 stamina, 300 mana (Qg), 126+ points spent. Fresh account HP 1500 / 1620 / 1740 / 1860 / 1980 / 2100 at lvls 1-6 (player put 4 points in HP per level). Consistent with 30 HP per point.
9. **Boss/enemy HP in non-map fights**: Worm Shaman 300 then 960 then 1560, Succubus 1512, "???" bosses 1060/1160/1272/1392/1400, Morden 1500 (lvl 6 account), and at lvl 50 enemy "Kasoi" 7140 HP, "Morden" 5750 HP with five cards level 23/26/29/26/27 (player "laitan" 5280 HP). These Morden/Kasoi fights are in the Pieces tab, not map Q97 (our Q97 HP 10,230 is therefore not contradicted). Rounds go up to RODADA/RONDA 10; negative overkill HP is shown (e.g. -9033 / 5280).
10. **Quest card drops are revealed face-up at 100 %** (Qg @ ~14:20: glowing card back then "Anvil Guard+++", "Rara", "Nivel 27", attack 2057, HP 2468, mana 13, text "Nao recebe dano na primeira rodada da luta"). Cards screen gets a red badge afterwards. Card shown in the card-detail panel has name + tier plusses, rarity word, 5 pips, level, ATK/HP/mana, magnifier button.

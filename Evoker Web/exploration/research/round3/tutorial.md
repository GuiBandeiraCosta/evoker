## 2. Animation / transition / layout details observed

- **Screen transitions**: fade through black (~0.3 s) between map/quest/cards; map -> quest uses a black fade; quest -> boss intro is a bright flash (white-gold burst expanding from the centre, ~0.5 s) revealing the rune circle; boss intro -> pre-battle slides the boss card up into the top row while the rune circle fades; Attack! -> battle is a quick dissolve.
- **Reward display**: gold = coin burst filling the landscape (coins fall with gravity), HUD gold counter increments to the new value over ~0.5 s; gems/potions appear as icons in the left "Rewards" column of the level-up panel; cards = "Card added to set: <Set name>" banner (with n/8 progress bar) + card rising into a glowing frame, tap = full-screen art, tap again = XP list "Level n" + **Continue** button.
- **Battle presentation**: header strip with boss portrait + name left and "ROUND n" right; enemy cards at the top, player cards below; hit = card flash and red floating damage number ("-100", "-200") over the HP bar; hero HP bar flashes red and its number shakes; attacks shown as a glowing fire bolt dropping vertically from the attacker; "Victory" = golden banner, "Defeat" = red banner with blood drips; no "Skip fight" until Q3.
- **Mid-fight speech**: guide and Shaman bubbles pop **over the player HP bar**, bottom-left portrait + bubble to the right.
- **HUD (top)**: "?" help button, level star + XP bar, orange stamina icon with `46/50`, blue mana icon `50/50`, small stat-level digits in front ("3" and "1" in 2014, "4","5" later), right block gold with a green "+", gems with a cyan gem icon. **No back arrow** in the HUD (our recreation has a back arrow at the far left).
- **Nav bar**: 6 square buttons: padlock, padlock, Quests (compass star), Cards (orange card fan), Shop (green, cards+plus), Character (orange up-arrow). A red "!" badge sits on the top-right corner of the Cards / Character button when something is new. **The Arena button replaces the 2nd padlock at Level 4 (crossed swords); the League button replaces the 1st padlock at Level 5 (trophy).**
- **Pre-battle screen**: only rows + Attack bar; **no HP bars, no drag tray, no "Boss Fight n" title** (our screenshot 07 shows "Boss Fight 1" header, HP bars 1,000 and 1,500, a player name "Smoke", and a "Your cards: drag a card into your deck" tray). Reordering/changing the deck happens on the Cards -> "Your Deck" tab.
- **Deck screen** ("Your Deck"): 5 slots (padlock where locked), under it "Your cards 2/40" (2014) / "Deine Karten 5/80" (2016) and a cyan bar "Mana needed: 5 / 50"; below the tab strip a horizontally scrolling tray of unplaced cards with small stat rows (attack red, health green, mana blue, "Level 1 / 10").

## 3. Comparison with our tutorial (game-design.md section 9, title.js, quest-map.js, quest.js, quests.js, state.js)

Sorted by importance for faithfulness.

1. **Opening missing / wrong.** Ours: Title -> name entry -> map. Original (2016): Flare logo -> **scripted unwinnable battle vs the Worm Shaman** (you: "Du", 1 card, 1500 HP; he: 3 cards, 300 HP) -> burning-tower cutscene -> map. No name prompt at start (auto name `user-<digits>`); the name is asked **at first Arena entry** (level 4) with the heralds text. Confidence high (Benito 0:08-0:30, `bz_0356_name_dialog`).
2. **Starter cards/deck**: ours = 6 cards (Envoy, Pikeman, Pouch Tearer, Bone Breaker, Scout, Master Brewer) with a pre-filled 3-card deck. Original: **2 cards** - Envoy (100/100/2) in deck slot, Master Brewer (100/200/3) shown via the "Dwarven Apprentice" collection pop-up; "Your cards 2/40". First fight is lost with Envoy alone; the player is taught to **drag Master Brewer into the deck**. (2016 build: opening fight with Envoy only.) High confidence.
3. **Quest 1 boss**: ours Pouch Tearer L1 + 2 commons, boss HP 1,000, no loss.

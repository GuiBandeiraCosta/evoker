== Special Ability ==
Djinn increases the healing power by (5 + his level)% of all your creatures until he dies.

Djinn's ability doesn't apply to player healers (e.g. [[Astral Navigator]]) or life increasers (e.g. [[Dwarven Sage]]).

Djinn's ability is applied to base healing power. Hence, for example, two level 45 Djinns increase the healing power by 100% (not 125%).

== Special Ability ==
At the end of a round, if Devourer's life is above zero, it will heal itself by some amount for each enemy card that died that round.

Example 1: A level 1 Devourer is hit for 1400 damage. At the end of the round three opposing cards die, so Devourer heals 1200 life (<span title="ability" style="border-bottom:1px dotted #000;">400</span> * <span title="# of dead opponents" style="border-bottom:1px dotted #000;">3</span> = 1200).

Example 2: A level 1 Devourer is hit for 2300 damage. At the end of the round two opposing cards die, but since Devourer is below 1 life he does not heal.

== Known Bugs ==
Occasionally, Devourer uses its healing ability even if it did not survive the round. Sometimes this is enough to keep it alive though it should have been dead. -> Devourer heals himself before receiving the damages from a "mirror" contrary to what is shown on the screen.

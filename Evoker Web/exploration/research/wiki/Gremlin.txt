== Special ability ==
Gremlins unique value lies in his mana cost: {{mana|1}}. It is the lowest mana cost of all cards. Because of this, Gremlin is the ideal card for "rerolling" your opponents using the [[Gremlin trick]].

== Tactics ==
Gremlin is a suicide blocker - he will save you from one hit for a minimal mana cost.

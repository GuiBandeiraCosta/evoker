== Special Ability ==

Dwarf King {{Strengthen}}s each of his neighbors by half of his ability.

'''Example:''' A Level 1 Dwarf King increases each neighbor's attack by 800, not 1600.

Dwarf King has the highest 2&nbsp;directional {{Strengthen}} ability.

== Special Ability ==
Dwarven Sage's health bonus is applied in the first round in which he is not [[suspend|suppressed]]. Once the bonus is applied, it persists even after Sage's death or suppression.

== History ==
Dwarven Sage's mana cost used to be 24 for short time after the appearance of The Dark Beyond portal.

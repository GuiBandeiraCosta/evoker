== Tactics ==

Tulzakk has a strong (unregular) attack pattern and acts as a support unit at the same time.

Regarding his ability, he can be successfully combined with cheap cards like the [[Basilisk]] and with cards with a spreading attack pattern, like most dragons.

Due to his attack pattern, he can defend or attack the edge, again in combinations with cheap [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Category:Suspender Suspender]/ [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Category:Dazer Dazer] or strong cards targeting a specific slot to pierce through, like [[Grimnack]].

The greatest weakness of Tulzakk is his comparatively low health. This and his splitted attack, and therefore lowered damage on his direct opponent, make him die more easily. This can be mitigated with covering cards, like [http://evoker.wikia.com/wiki/Category:Healer Healer‏‎]s and the [[Swarmkeeper]] or even leveraged with the [[Moon Dragon]] to revive him.

All in all, Tulzakks is a strong attacking card, with great flexibility. He wont last as long as a [[Giant Spider]], [[Chimera]] or [[Griffin]], but often has done his job anyway when the match is over or he dies.<!--VERTICAL WHITE SPACE BELOW-->

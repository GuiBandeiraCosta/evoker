== Overview ==
Evey day, each player receives a reward proportional to number of consecutive days he/she stayed in the game.

== Technical description ==
The game keeps a counter that counts the number of consecutive days (up to present) when you logged in. This counter is set to 1 when you create your account. It is updated at the end of every day (days start at 0:00 of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenwich_Mean_Time GMT]). It is set to 0 at the end of a day if you didn't log in that day. It is increased by 1 if you logged in that day. On the first login of a day, you receive "Day D" reward, where D is the value of the counter.

'''Example:''' On 31st March, you didn't log into the game. You log in on 1st April receiving Day 1 reward, and again on 2nd April receiving Day 2 reward. Then you don't log in on 3rd. Upon logging in on 4th, you receive Day 1 reward.

== Special Ability ==
When Warg Rider's death ability triggers, he hits opposing cards left, direct, right and also his own left and right neighbors with X/2 damage each, where X is his ''basic'' power (that is, without any strengthening effects). If some of the slots are unoccupied, the damage is dealt to the corresponding player.

== Questions ==
If Warg Rider is placed in the leftmost or rightmost slot, what happens with the death damage going over the edge?

Answer: Much like goblin patriarch the damage is nullified on the side in which it goes over the edge.

== Overview ==
Gremlin trick is a way to avoid losing mana in combats against obviously more powerful opponents.

== Description ==
If you are paired against an opponent you don't want to fight (probably because he is too powerful), simply change your army so that it consist of a single low mana card - preferably [[Gremlin]], which has the lowest mana cost amongst all cards. Then attack the opponent. The battle will be lost, but only minimum of mana consumed and you will be paired to another random opponent. You can't be paired to the previous opponent for several hours.

By repeating this trick, you often grab a few cardpieces for free, since players sometimes leave their cardpieces unguarded (to get rid of unwanted ones).

Notice that by using this trick too often, you will eventually run out of opponents possessing the desired cardpiece. This is usually not an issue for Main Quest cards, which are always plentiful. But it is a real problem for portal cards, especially if only few player are active (some portals are less interesting than others).

This trick is useful only in cardpiece combat, since your opponents in Arena are rerolled automatically.

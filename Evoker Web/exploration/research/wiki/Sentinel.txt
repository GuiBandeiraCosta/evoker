== Special Ability ==

=== Healing ===
Sentinel has a unique healing ability. It heals each of your other cards, regardless of their tribe, each round. The only other card with a similar ability is [[Herb Collector]], which heals only other elves.

=== Indestructible ===
Sentinel also doesn't take any damage in the first round.
